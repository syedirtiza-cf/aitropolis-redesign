Healthcare operations at scale

Major healthcare provider managing multiple hospitals, clinics, and research facilities couldn't leverage data effectively. Patient outcomes, operational efficiency, and predictive care were siloed across systems.

  • Fragmented patient data across systems
  • No predictive analytics for outcomes
  • Manual operational planning
  • Limited interoperability between providers
The Solution

04 / 07

AI-powered care delivery

AI digital twin integrating patient data, clinical workflows, and operational metrics across AWS, Azure, and Vertex AI for unified intelligence.

  • Predictive clinical outcomes
  • Real-time resource optimisation
  • AI-driven decision support
