The Challenge

Legacy systems holding back growth

A Fortune 500 enterprise was struggling with fragmented, aging systems across finance, supply chain, and operations. Manual processes, data silos, and integration nightmares were costing millions annually and limiting scalability.

  • Fragmented data across 12 legacy systems
  • Manual reconciliation taking 40+ FTE hours weekly
  • Zero real-time visibility into operations
  • Unable to scale to new markets

The Solution

Unified platform for enterprise scale

We implemented a best-of-breed ERP solution (Oracle/D365) with custom integrations, change management, and 24/7 support across 4 global studios.

  • Single source of truth across functions
  • Automated workflows reducing manual work by 85%
  • Real-time dashboards for decision-makers
  • Multi-currency, multi-entity support
