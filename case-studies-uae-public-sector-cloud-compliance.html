A Dubai government entity had to meet UAE IAR security compliance while its in-house team was already carrying a heavy functional operations workload.

The Challenge

Compliance requirements, limited in-house capacity

The entity needed to meet UAE Information Assurance Regulation security standards while its in-house team carried a heavy functional operations workload.

  • UAE IAR information security compliance requirement
  • Heavy in-house functional resource burden
  • Need for consistent SLA-backed operations
  • Public-sector risk and audit requirements

The Solution

Oracle Cloud migration with managed operations

Aitropolis migrated the environment to Oracle Cloud to satisfy IAR compliance requirements, then took on day-to-day operations under an SLA-based managed services model.

  • Oracle Cloud migration for IAR compliance
  • SLA-based managed services model
  • Reduced in-house functional workload
  • Ongoing compliance-aligned operations
