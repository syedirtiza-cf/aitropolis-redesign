Questions

Private Cloud, answered

When is private cloud better than public cloud?

When data residency or regulation limits where a workload may run, when latency to on-premises systems matters, or when steady, predictable load makes owned capacity cheaper.

VMware or OpenShift?

VMware suits virtual-machine estates. OpenShift suits container-based applications. Many clients run both, and we advise on where each belongs.

Can a private cloud connect to public cloud?

Yes. Hybrid designs are common, with the private platform holding regulated data and public cloud taking burst or new workloads.

Can you operate it after the build?

Yes, under a managed service agreement, or we hand over to your team with documentation.
