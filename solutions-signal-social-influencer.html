Influencer Marketing & Social Management: what is included

Creator selection

Shortlists built from audience fit, content quality and past brand work, not follower count alone.

Briefs and contracts

Clear deliverables, usage rights, approval steps and payment terms agreed before anything is published.

Disclosure and compliance

Paid partnerships labelled as the platforms and advertising rules in your market require.

Social media management

Day-to-day publishing, community replies and reporting for the channels where your buyers are.

B2B and B2C programmes

LinkedIn voices and industry experts for business audiences, and consumer creators for retail and lifestyle.

Measurement

Tracked links and codes tied to leads and sales, reported next to your other channels.

How it runs

02 / 05

From first call to hand-over

  1. Step 1: Define the buyer

    Agree who you need to reach and what action counts as success.

  2. Step 2: Shortlist

    Vet creators against that buyer and check their audience before outreach.

  3. Step 3: Brief and agree

    Set deliverables, rights, disclosure and tracking in writing.

  4. Step 4: Run

    Manage content approvals, publishing and the social channels around the campaign.

  5. Step 5: Measure and repeat

    Report against the agreed action and keep the creators who delivered it.

Questions

Influencer Marketing & Social Management, answered

How do you choose creators?

We start from your buyer, then check audience fit, content quality and past brand work. Follower count is one input and not the deciding one.

Do you manage our social accounts too?

Yes. Social media management and influencer programmes can run as one engagement or separately.

How do you measure results?

Through tracked links, discount codes and landing pages tied to leads and sales, reported alongside your other channels.

How is this different from your social media marketing service?

Social media marketing covers your own channels and content. This service adds paid creator partnerships and their management.
