Enterprise Blockchain

02 / 07

Why blockchain matters to enterprise

Immutability

Once recorded, data cannot be altered. Audit trails are permanent and verifiable.

Transparency

All participants see the same ledger. No hidden transactions or disputes.

Smart Contracts

Automate complex business logic. Contracts execute when conditions are met.

Decentralization

No single point of failure. Consensus-based decisions, not centralised control.

Interoperability

Blockchains can communicate with legacy systems and other chains.

Compliance

Built-in audit trails satisfy regulatory requirements. Permissions and access control.
