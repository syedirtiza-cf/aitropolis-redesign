What we do

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or voice control. We design and test against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA.

  • Semantic HTML with one h1 per page, landmarks, and a “skip to content” link.
  • Every interactive element is reachable and operable by keyboard and shows a designed, high-contrast focus ring.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
  • Text and interface colours meet the contrast ratios below. An automated check runs before every release and fails the build if any pair drops below its threshold.
  • Icons are decorative and hidden from assistive technology, or carry a text label.
  • Motion is limited to short transitions and stops if you have asked your device to reduce motion.
  • Content, figures and times are rendered on the server, so they are present without JavaScript.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are described in text, not colour alone.

Known limitations

  • Some older case-study pages contain dense figure tables that are being restructured for easier reading with a screen reader.
  • The booking page is provided by a third party. We have asked them to confirm its conformance, and you can always contact us to book instead.

Report a barrier

If something on the site does not work for you, email uk@aitropolis.co.uk with the page address and what happened. We will reply within 5 working days and, where we can, provide the information in another format.
