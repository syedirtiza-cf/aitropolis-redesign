Success Stories

05 / 08

Real ERP transformations

200+ successful implementations across banking, manufacturing, government, telecom, retail, and healthcare.

UAE Bank (Anonymous)

Timeline: Phased rollout

Challenge

Consolidate multiple banking subsidiaries onto a single Oracle E-Business Suite platform under strict regulatory compliance (CBUAE, international accounting standards) and a tight deadline.

Solution

Phased implementation across GL, AR, and AP. Configured a multi-entity architecture with automated inter-company eliminations. Built custom forms for regulatory reporting, with a parallel run against the legacy system before cutover.

Results

On-time go-live with a clean compliance audit. Consolidated financial reporting replaced a patchwork of manual reconciliations, and AR/AP processing became materially faster.

  • Banking & Financial Services

Middle East Industrial Conglomerate

Timeline: Multi-phase rollout

Challenge

Migrate from EBS 11i to Oracle Cloud (Fusion) with complex bill-of-materials and project-based job costing, across a large multi-location user base.

Solution

Greenfield Fusion setup on Oracle Cloud. Re-engineered manufacturing processes for a cloud-native approach, implemented the Project Costing module, and rolled out training via a centralised learning management system.

Results

Improved manufacturing lead times and job-costing accuracy, lower infrastructure overhead with no on-prem hardware to maintain, and global visibility into supply chain and production.

  • Manufacturing & Heavy Industries

GCC Government Ministry

Timeline: Multi-agency phased rollout

Challenge

Implement Oracle EBS across multiple agencies with budget constraints, strict audit-trail requirements, and a largely non-technical user base.

Solution

Centralised EBS with separate GL codes per agency, automated workflow approvals based on organisational hierarchy, Arabic-language dashboards and reports, and a phased change-management rollout.

Results

Unified financial reporting across agencies, improved budget transparency, and a significantly shorter audit cycle.

  • Government & Public Sector

Regional Telecom Operator

Timeline: Structured cutover window

Challenge

Replace a legacy billing and ERP system supporting a large subscriber base with complex billing rules and revenue-assurance requirements, with no tolerance for downtime.

Solution

Parallel-run strategy with automated reconciliation, an Oracle Telecom module configured for custom billing rules, and real-time integration with OSS/BSS systems during a tightly scoped cutover window.

Results

Cutover completed with no revenue loss, improved billing accuracy, and revenue-assurance controls that surfaced previously hidden leakage.

  • Telecom & Technology

MENA Retail Conglomerate

Timeline: Pre-peak-season rollout

Challenge

Centralise inventory and financials across a large multi-store network with real-time point-of-sale integration, deployed ahead of peak holiday season with zero tolerance for downtime.

Solution

Oracle EBS paired with a warehouse management system, real-time POS integration via middleware, store-manager training on inventory workflows, and automated replenishment based on sales velocity.

Results

Lower inventory carrying costs, fewer stock-outs, better store-level profitability visibility, and a clean run through peak holiday volume.

  • Retail & Distribution

Large Hospital Group

Timeline: Multi-facility rollout

Challenge

Implement ERP for multi-facility healthcare operations, covering inventory tracking, pharmaceutical compliance, and financial consolidation.

Solution

Oracle EBS with healthcare-specific materials management for medications, lot/serial tracking for pharma compliance, and separate cost centers per facility.

Results

Improved medication inventory accuracy, a clean compliance audit, and better supply-chain visibility across facilities.

  • Healthcare & Pharma
