The 4 Use Cases

Blockchain solves specific problems where the value is in provenance, immutability, or decentralization.

1. Supply Chain Provenance

Track goods from manufacture to end customer. Prevents counterfeits. Luxury, pharma, and high-value manufacturing save 15-30% on fraud.

2. Asset Tokenisation

Represent real assets (art, real estate, securities) as digital tokens. Unlocks fractional ownership and faster settlement.

3. Smart Contract Automation

Multi-party contracts (insurance claims, vendor payments, warranty fulfillment) execute automatically when conditions met. No intermediary, faster settlement.

4. Compliance & Audit Trails

Immutable records for regulated industries (banking, healthcare, government). Reduces audit cost by 20-40%.

When NOT to Use Blockchain

  • - Payment processing (use traditional rails)
  • - High-frequency trading (too slow)
  • - Public data that doesn't need permanence
