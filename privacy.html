What we collect

This policy explains what personal data Aitropolis Technologies Ltd (“we”) collects through this website, why, how long we keep it and the rights you have. We are the controller of that data. Our registered address is London, United Kingdom.

We do not collect special-category data through this website and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

  • Enquiries. When you use the contact form we receive your name, email address and message, and, if you give them, your phone number, company and the service you are interested in.
  • Bookings. When you book an assessment, the booking provider collects your name, email and the slot you choose, and passes them to us.
  • Usage data. With analytics enabled we record pages viewed, referring site, approximate location (country or city, derived from an anonymised IP address), device and browser type.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We never sell personal data.

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare for a meeting you asked for: steps taken at your request before a contract.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it: our legitimate interests, or your consent where the law requires it.
  • To measure advertising, where an advertising tag is enabled: your consent.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations: legal obligation.

Who we share it with

Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract: our hosting and email provider, the booking provider and the analytics providers above. Where those providers are outside the UK or the UAE, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a project are deleted within 24 months of our last contact. Client records are kept for as long as accounting and contractual obligations require. Analytics data is retained for 14 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the UK GDPR and the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to uk@aitropolis.co.uk. We reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data-protection regulator; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it to respond. Our information security programme is working towards ISO 27001 certification.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top will change with it. For other Aitropolis Technologies entities, contact info@aitropolis.com.
