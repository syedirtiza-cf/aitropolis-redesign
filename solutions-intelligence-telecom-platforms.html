Questions

Telecom Platforms · Answered

What's the difference between OSS and BSS?

OSS (Operations Support Systems) manages the network: provisioning, configuration, fault management, and optimisation. BSS (Business Support Systems) manages the customer: billing, orders, customer care, and revenue. We build both, integrated into one platform.

Can you migrate from legacy telecom systems?

Yes. We've migrated carriers from Amdocs, Openet, and home-grown systems to cloud-native platforms with zero downtime. Typical migration window: 3-6 months with parallel running and gradual cutover.

Do you support MVNO use cases?

Yes. We build white-label MVNO platforms: subscriber management, rating & billing, IVR systems, and self-care portals. API-first architecture allows easy integration with host carrier systems.

How does AI improve telecom platforms?

Churn prediction to proactively retain customers. Revenue assurance to detect leakage. Network optimisation to reduce congestion. Fraud detection to prevent SIM swaps and unauthorized roaming. Self-care optimisation to reduce customer support load.
