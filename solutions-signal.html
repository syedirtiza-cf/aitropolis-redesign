Why AI, Why Now

AI doesn't replace growth teams. It gives them a better signal.

We use AI across the growth lifecycle — from market and customer intelligence to content generation, campaign optimisation, experimentation, lead scoring and revenue analytics. The result is a growth system that learns continuously instead of relying on quarterly guesses.

Funnel

A growth loop, not a funnel that ends

Customer behaviour creates new signals. Every stage feeds the next — and the last stage feeds back into the first.

  1. Step 1: Discover

    Find the market, audience and problem worth solving.

  2. Step 2: Attract

    Earn attention through SEO, content, paid and social.

  3. Step 3: Activate

    Turn attention into trials, sign-ups and first value.

  4. Step 4: Convert

    Turn intent into revenue with sales-ready pipeline.

  5. Step 5: Retain

    Keep customers engaged and reduce churn.

  6. Step 6: Expand

    Grow account value through upsell and advocacy.

  7. Step 7: Learn

    Feed customer and market signals back into discovery.

Our Process

How we build growth
