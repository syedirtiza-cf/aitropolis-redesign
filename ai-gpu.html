Eight-GPU HGX server

H200, B200 or B300

Workload profile:Large-model training, high-throughput inference and multi-node clusters

  • Eight SXM GPUs on an NVIDIA HGX baseboard
  • NVLink and NVSwitch between every GPU
  • SuperNICs for InfiniBand or 400G/800G Ethernet
  • Enterprise NVMe and a path to shared parallel storage
  • Out-of-band management and rack integration

09 / 11

Agents, put into production.

Claims and document processing, KYC review, support triage, procurement and engineering agents, deployed with the four controls that decide whether they survive real work.

The honest limitation: an agent is only as good as the evaluation set you can write for it. Where a task has no checkable right answer, we scope it as assistance rather than automation.

  • Evaluation harness
  • A scored test set the agent has to pass before it touches production, and again after every change.
  • Guardrails
  • Hard limits on what the agent may call, spend, write to and see, enforced outside the model.
  • Human in the loop
  • A review step on the decisions that carry cost or risk.
  • Rollback
  • A way back to the previous behaviour that does not depend on the agent agreeing to it.
Questions

Answers, on the record.

Do you sell H200, B200 and B300 servers?

Yes. We supply NVIDIA H200, B200 and B300 systems, in four-GPU and eight-GPU HGX configurations, along with RTX professional cards and the networking and storage around them. We source each order against the configuration rather than hold stock, so every quote names the configuration and its lead time.

Which GPU is right for my workload?

It depends on model size, memory per GPU, training or inference, throughput, power and budget. H200 suits large-memory AI and HPC work, B200 is Blackwell training and inference, and B300 gives the most memory per system. Send us the workload and we recommend a configuration.

What is included in a GPU server?

More than the GPUs: CPUs, system memory, NVMe, the NVLink/NVSwitch interconnect, NICs or SuperNICs, power, cooling and rack configuration, then the operating system, NVIDIA drivers, CUDA, container runtime, orchestration and monitoring. We specify all of it, which is why we do not compete on GPU unit price alone.

Can you source GPU hardware without the deployment work?

Yes. Send a part number or a rough configuration and you get a specification and a lead time back. Nothing obliges you to buy the deployment alongside it.

Where do you deliver and operate?

From studios in Dubai, London, Islamabad, Singapore, serving clients across 30+ countries. We respond within 24 hours on business days.
