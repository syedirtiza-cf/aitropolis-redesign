Questions

Offshore teams, answered

Where are Aitropolis's offshore development centers located?

Aitropolis operates dedicated offshore development centers in Islamabad, Pakistan, and Singapore, giving clients coverage across South Asia and APAC timezones alongside our Dubai and London studios.

What engagement models are available for offshore teams?

Three models: Dedicated Team (a fixed team of engineers working exclusively on your product, managed by Aitropolis), Staff Augmentation (individual engineers embedded directly in your existing team and processes), and Project-Based Development (a scoped deliverable with a fixed timeline and outcome).

How are engineers vetted before joining a client team?

Every engineer goes through technical screening, live coding assessment, and a communication/culture-fit interview before being presented to a client. Only pre-vetted engineers are placed on client engagements.

Do offshore teams work in our timezone?

Yes. Teams align their working hours to overlap with your core hours, and our follow-the-sun model across Dubai, London, Islamabad, and Singapore means someone from your extended team is reachable close to around the clock.

Who manages quality and delivery for offshore engagements?

Aitropolis provides full management oversight - a delivery manager, code review standards, sprint cadence, and reporting - so offshore capacity behaves like an extension of your team, not a black box.
