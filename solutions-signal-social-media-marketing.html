Frequently Asked

08 / 08

Common questions

How long before we see results?

Community growth and engagement typically show movement within 4-6 weeks. Revenue-attributable results depend on sales cycle length, but paid campaigns can show conversion signal within the first 30 days.

Which platforms do you recommend for us?

It depends on your audience and goals. We run a platform audit as part of the Discover phase and recommend a focused mix rather than spreading thin across every channel.

Do you produce the creative content yourselves?

Yes. Our in-house team handles copywriting, graphic design, video editing, and photography, along with sourcing when specialised production is required.

Can you work alongside our existing marketing team?

Yes. We integrate with in-house teams and other agencies, operating as your social media specialists within a broader marketing function.
